How it works

17 live effects: Normal, Grayscale, Sepia, Invert, Vintage, Noir, Neon, Comic, Pixelate, Blur, Glitch, Mirror Split, Rainbow, Posterize, Thermal, Edge Sketch and Kaleidoscope.

Recording: Pause/resume to create cuts while recording. The video cutter can trim uploaded videos or newly saved recordings (export from the cutter is silent).

Browser notes: Chrome or Edge on desktop is recommended. Camera permissions require a secure page (HTTPS) or http://localhost. Some browsers may not support recording, export, or some source video formats.

Camera Lab — runs in your browser. No uploads or external libraries.
